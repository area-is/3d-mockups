---
title: Introduction
description: GPU-accelerated 3D device mockups for React, with real live DOM on every screen.
---

`area-mockups` renders procedurally generated 3D devices with WebGL and maps
real, live DOM onto their screens: text stays vector crisp at any angle,
videos play, iframes load, and React state and effects keep running. There are
no 3D asset files to host and nothing to download at runtime.

## Highlights

- **Seventeen devices**: the Galaxy S26 line (S26, S26 Ultra), the Galaxy
  Z Fold 7 and Z Flip 7 foldables, the full iPhone 17 family (17, 17 Air,
  17 Pro, 17 Pro Max), MacBook Air 13" and MacBook Pro 14" (M5), iPad Pro 13"
  and 11" (M5), Galaxy Tab S11 and S11 Ultra, an Apple Watch Series 11 and
  Galaxy Watch 8 wearing full wristbands, and a Studio Display style 27"
  monitor.
- **Twenty-three objects**: hardcover book, magazine, tri-fold brochure,
  business card, ID badge on a lanyard, greeting card, gallery poster frame,
  retail product box, corrugated mailer box, kraft shopping bag,
  **custom-size panels and boxes** (any millimeter dimensions),
  vinyl record, roll-up banner, A-frame sidewalk sign, bus shelter,
  double-sided DOOH totem, 14'×48' billboard, a high-street storefront, a
  city transit bus, a wrap-ready cargo van, a 53 ft semi trailer, and a
  65" TV — the printable surface of each is a live DOM area.
- **True-to-device screens**: each virtual display defaults to the real device's
  logical resolution in portrait and landscape, so your layouts and breakpoints
  behave exactly like on the hardware.
- **Real GPU rendering**: three.js and react-three-fiber, physically based
  materials, studio lighting, soft shadows, clamped device pixel ratio.
- **Any content on screen**: pass React components, an `<iframe>`, or a
  `<video>` as children. Pointer events, state, and scrolling keep working.
- **Composable**: use the one-liner mockup components, or drop the bare devices
  into your own react-three-fiber scene.

## Installation

```bash
npm install area-mockups three @react-three/fiber @react-three/drei
```

React 18 or newer (19 recommended). `three`, `@react-three/fiber`, and
`@react-three/drei` are peer dependencies.
