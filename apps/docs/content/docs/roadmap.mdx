---
title: Roadmap
description: Where the library is heading next.
---

- More devices: more monitors, more wearables, and desktop towers.
- More objects: cans and bottles, apparel, more vehicles, and multi-page
  spreads (open book and magazine interiors).
- Deep-link camera poses and scroll-driven animation helpers.

TVs, foldables and packaging (product box, mailer box, shopping bag, custom
boxes) were on this list and have since shipped. See
[Devices](/docs/devices) and [Objects](/docs/objects).
