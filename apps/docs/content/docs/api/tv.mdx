`<TVSet>` and `<TVSetMockup>` render a modern flat-screen (65" by default,
any diagonal from 32" to 98" via `size`) in one of three designs, each
proportioned from the published dimensions of current Samsung and LG sets.
The screen is a live 1920×1080 DOM surface - full desktop layouts,
showreels, and `<video>` all apply.

Sizing follows real product ranges rather than uniform scaling: the panel
scales with the diagonal, while the bezels, cabinet depth, ports and stand
keep hardware dimensions - feet hold a near-constant inset from the panel
ends and grow only mildly across the range.

## Props

Beyond the [shared screen props](/docs/screen-content):

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `variant` | `'legs' \| 'pedestal' \| 'frame'` | `'legs'` | Which design: splayed A-frame feet, a center pedestal, or the wall-hung picture-frame set. |
| `size` | `number` | `65` | Diagonal in inches, clamped to 32–98. Panel scales; bezels, ports and stand follow real product ratios. |
| `color` | `string` | `'#15171b'` | Enclosure colorway (frame, back, stand). |
| `resolution` | `number` | `1920` | Virtual display width; 1920 gives 1920×1080. |

There is no `orientation` prop; it lives on a media stand.

## The three designs

### `legs` - the wide 4K set

A near-bezel-less panel (~7.6 mm frame, ~14.6 mm chin), thin at the edges
with a shallow electronics bulge low on the back, a recessed rear input bay
(HDMI ×3, USB ×2, LAN, optical audio, antenna coax), and two feet near the
ends - each a pair of wide-splayed struts, the shallow Λ stance of current
retail stands.

### `pedestal` - the Neo QLED on a center plate

Proportioned from Samsung's QN70F spec sheet. At 75": 1677.5 × 960.7 ×
26.6 mm of cabinet over a 1660.6 × 934.1 mm panel - an 8.45 mm frame and an
18.15 mm chin on a 26.6 mm AirSlim slab. One slim neck drops from the
cabinet onto the AERO CENTER plate, lifting the panel 56.8 mm and letting
the set fit furniture far narrower than itself. Samsung publishes that
plate at four diagonals (55": 263.9 × 247.4 mm → 85": 375.4 × 366.5 mm),
so `size` interpolates between them instead of scaling one footprint.

### `frame` - the picture-frame set

Proportioned from the 65" Samsung The Frame LS03F: 1458 × 833 × 25.4 mm
over the same panel, which works out to a **uniform ~14.75 mm bezel with no
chin** on a slab of even thickness (its electronics live in an external
connect box, so there is no bulge and no rear bay). It ships with the Slim
Fit Wall Mount and hangs flush, so it stands on nothing - the panel meets
the surface directly. Set `color` to one of the bezel finishes -
`#1b1c1e` black, `#e8e4dd` white, `#a9825a` teak, `#6d5341` brown.
