---
title: Watch (Apple Watch + Galaxy Watch)
description: Apple Watch Series 11 on a seamless Solo Loop, or Galaxy Watch 8 on a buckled band.
---

`<Watch>` and `<WatchMockup>` render a smartwatch wearing a real wristband on
an invisible wrist directly behind the case — two domed, tapering straps
sliding into the band slots in the case edges and meeting at a closure on the
underside of the wrist, matching how worn-band product photos pose it.

The two devices wear different band constructions. The Apple Watch gets the
**Solo Loop**: one continuous stretchy band, sized to the wrist rather than
adjusted, so there is no closure, no hole row and no hardware anywhere on it.
The Galaxy Watch gets a two-strap band whose length is taken from the retail
fit range rather than eyeballed, closing with a stainless pin buckle over a
row of holes punched clean through the long strap.

Set `bandOpen` to lay the Galaxy's band out instead of wearing it. A worn band
is a closed loop, and the far side of a closed loop sits between the camera and
the watch — so it always hides the case back. Open, the straps run out on a
wide near-flat arc and the hole row, the buckle and the sensor back all face
the viewer, the way a band is photographed off-wrist. It has no effect on the
Apple Watch: a seamless loop has no closure to undo.

Both cases carry their real back: an optical heart-sensor cluster behind a
round crystal ringed by the ECG electrode — sunk flush into Apple's
body-colour aluminium plate, raised on Samsung's BioActive puck.

Two variants at true relative sizes:

- **`series11`** (default): Apple Watch Series 11, 46 mm — squircle case,
  gear-toothed knurled Digital Crown, flush side button in its machined
  recess, drilled mic hole, machined speaker slot on the left edge, the
  **Solo Loop** — one seamless stretchy band with no closure, no holes and no
  hardware, flaring into the lug slots at both ends — and a live 208×248
  rounded-rect face.
- **`watch8`**: Samsung Galaxy Watch 8, 44 mm — cushion case with the fully
  **round** 1.47" display raised on its dial puck (live 240×240 circular
  face), two raised chamfered keys straddling the mic hole, twin machined
  speaker slots on the left edge, and a Dynamic-Lug-style band: nearly
  case-wide at the connector, tapering around the wrist to a stainless pin
  buckle whose tongue rises through one of the punched holes, plus a keeper
  holding the tail down.

## Props

Beyond the [shared screen props](/docs/screen-content):

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `variant` | `'series11' \| 'watch8'` | `'series11'` | Which watch to render. |
| `color` | `string` | `'#1c1d21'` | Case colorway. |
| `bandColor` | `string` | `'#2a2c31'` | Strap (fluoroelastomer). |
| `bandOpen` | `boolean` | `false` | Lay the band open instead of wearing it: the straps run out on a wide flat arc, so the hole row, buckle and case back all face the camera. `watch8` only — the Apple's Solo Loop is seamless. |
| `resolution` | `number` | per variant | Virtual display width: 208 (Apple point grid) or 240 (Galaxy dp grid). |

There is no `orientation` prop.
