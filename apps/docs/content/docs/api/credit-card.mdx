title: Credit card
description: A payment card with an EMV chip, an embossed number, name and expiry, a magnetic stripe, and live front and back faces.

`<CreditCard>` and `<CreditCardMockup>` render an ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1
payment card - 85.60×53.98 mm on 0.76 mm PVC with 3.18 mm corners - with a
live 520×328 DOM surface on the front and, optionally, the back. Your art
prints full bleed under a gloss laminate that catches the studio lights as
the card turns; over it sit the parts a real card carries on top of its
print, each placed where the standards put it:

- the **EMV chip**: a 13×11.4 mm gold or silver contact plate in its milled
  cavity over the ISO/IEC 7816-2 contact field, its contacts etched into
  the plate, essentially flush with the card;
- the **embossed number, name and expiry**: real raised relief, not a
  texture, up to 0.46 mm high (ISO/IEC 7811-1). Each stroke is a flat
  crest, where the silver or gold foil tipping is stamped, on shoulders
  that slope down into the print. The number is set in a Farrington
  7B-style face, centred 21.42 mm above the bottom edge; the expiry and the
  name below it, in the name-and-address area, in the smaller gothic with a
  printed "VALID THRU" legend. They can be printed flat instead;
- on the back, the embossing's **mirrored, debossed impressions**, the
  **magnetic stripe** (12.7 mm tall, 5.54 mm below the top edge) and the
  **signature panel**;
- round it all, the **white core** that shows at the cut edge of a
  laminated card.

## Props

Beyond the [shared screen props](/docs/screen-content):

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `color` | `string` | `'#1f2b46'` | Card stock: an unprinted back, and the print's ground wherever the art leaves it clear. |
| `edgeColor` | `string` | `'#f1efe9'` | The cut edge. A PVC card's printed sheets are laminated over a white core, so its edge shows white whatever the print; set this for a coloured core or a metal card. |
| `number` | `string` | `'4000 1234 5678 9010'` | The embossed card number. Spaces are kept as typed; a number too long for the line condenses to fit. |
| `name` | `string` | `'ALEX MORGAN'` | The embossed cardholder name, along the bottom of the card. |
| `expiry` | `string` | `'12/29'` | The embossed expiry, with a printed "VALID THRU" legend to its left. `''` leaves both out. |
| `emboss` | `boolean \| 'flat'` | `true` | How the number, name and expiry are set. `true` raises them (and presses their impressions into the back); `'flat'` prints them in a thin line with no relief, the way most cards issued today do; `false` leaves them off, for art that sets its own. |
| `tipping` | `'silver' \| 'gold' \| 'none' \| string` | `'silver'` | Foil on the embossed crests: a preset, any CSS color, or `'none'` for untipped crests that show the print they were pushed up through. With `emboss="flat"` it is the colour the lines print in, and `'none'` prints them in plain ink. |
| `chip` | `boolean \| 'gold' \| 'silver'` | `true` | The EMV chip's contact plate on the front: `'gold'` (or `true`), `'silver'`, or `false` for none. |
| `finish` | `'gloss' \| 'matte'` | `'gloss'` | The laminate over the print. `'gloss'` catches the studio's highlights as the card turns; `'matte'` keeps the print flat, for soft-touch cards. |
| `stripe` | `boolean` | `true` | The magnetic stripe across the top of the back. |
| `signature` | `boolean` | `true` | The signature panel on the back, under the stripe. |
| `surfaceBackground` | `string` | `color` | CSS background behind each face's content - the stock itself unless you set it, so whatever the content leaves clear prints on `color`. |
| `resolution` | `number` | `520` | Virtual face width in CSS px (~150 dpi); height follows the card aspect. |

The embossed lines take capitals, digits, spaces and `/`, `-` and `.`.
Lower case is raised as capitals and accented letters lose their accents
(José becomes JOSE), the way a card bureau transliterates a name; anything
else is left out, and in development the console names what was dropped,
once per character.

## Regions

| Slot | Surface |
| --- | --- |
| `<CreditCardMockup.Front>` | Front face, full bleed (bare children). |
| `<CreditCardMockup.Back>` | Back face, full bleed - plain stock when omitted. |

Every slot takes per-surface overrides - `surfaceBackground`,
`resolution`, `surfaceStyle` - over the mockup-level defaults. The
same slots exist on the bare `<CreditCard>`.

The chip, the embossing, the stripe and the signature panel are 3D
hardware over the art, so they cover whatever you print under them,
pixel for pixel. Lay the art out around them - the chip's cavity is 7.9 to
21.6 mm in from the left and 16.6 to 28.7 mm down, the number line 30.4 to
34.7 mm down - or switch the piece off and print your own.

## How the embossing is built

No font file is loaded. The lettering is a small stroke font in the core -
every glyph a few polylines on a 4×6 grid (`STROKE_FONT`), laid out by
`layoutStrokeText` - and the card sweeps each stroke the way a die shapes
PVC: a flat crest half the stroke wide, on shoulders that fall away on a
smooth curve and meet the card with no seam. Each is one merged geometry
per line, rebuilt only when that line's text changes. The foil is a
part-metal film on the crests only, so they catch the studio lights the way
a real card's do, while the shoulders show the print they were pushed up
through, lit and shaded, and a soft contact shadow grounds each stroke.
Untipped crests, and the impressions on the back, are drawn as shading over
the live print in the same way, because that is what they are: the art
itself, pushed up or pressed in. Printed flat, the same strokes are laid on
the face as a thin monoline.

```ts
import { CREDIT_CARD, layoutStrokeText } from 'react-3d-mockups/core'

// The number line's strokes, as [x, y] polylines in world units on the face.
const { strokes, bounds } = layoutStrokeText('4000 1234 5678 9010', CREDIT_CARD.emboss.number)
```

### Printed flat

Most cards issued today print the number, name and expiry rather than
emboss them. `emboss="flat"` sets the same lines in a thin printed line
with no relief and nothing pressed into the back; `tipping` picks the ink.
Here on a matte card with a silver chip.
