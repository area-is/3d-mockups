title: Van (cargo van)
description: A wrap-ready cargo van with a live livery panel on the cargo side.
---

`<Van>` and `<VanMockup>` render a generic Euro-style cargo van (a
Transit/Sprinter-class silhouette, no brand): the shell is the side profile —
clamshell hood, cowl break, raked windshield, high roof, wheel-arch cutouts —
extruded across the width, with six-lug steel wheels, A-pillar-raked door
glass, parked windshield wipers, cab-door shut lines, a sliding-door entry
step, pressed roof ribs, a stub antenna, a wide slatted grille between
headlights under the hood line, fog recesses in the bumper corners,
license-plate recesses front and rear, barn-door hinges, mirrors on long
arms and hand-height taillights added on. The flat cargo side carries a
live 900×368 vinyl-wrap panel for your livery. The mockup grounds the wheels
on a virtual road plane.

<ObjectDemo demo="van-basic" checker />

```tsx
<VanMockup rotation={[0, -0.5, 0]}>
  <Livery />
  <VanMockup.StreetSide><Livery /></VanMockup.StreetSide>
  <VanMockup.Rear><RearDoors /></VanMockup.Rear>
  <VanMockup.LicensePlate>AREA 51</VanMockup.LicensePlate>
</VanMockup>
```

## Props

Beyond the [shared screen props](/docs/screen-content):

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `color` | `string` | `'#eef0f2'` | Body paint. Wrap fleets are usually white. |
| `surfaceBackground` | `string` | `'#ffffff'` | CSS background behind your wrap content. |
| `coverage` | `'panel' \| 'full' \| 'perforated'` | `'panel'` | `'full'` covers the entire side elevations AND the whole rear face — door edge to door edge — carving out the wheel arches and door glass, while the proud hardware (mirrors, handles, door track, cab-door shut lines) draws on top of the livery via per-pixel depth compositing, like hardware remounted over the vinyl. On the rear, every taillight lamp, the third brake light and the hinge knuckles are carved to a slim install margin. Both coverages keep the barn-door shut line visible through the wrap. `'perforated'` is the same full wrap running OVER the cab door glass as perforated window film. |
| `resolution` | `number` | `900` panel · `1360` full | Virtual side-wrap width in CSS px; the rear panel shares its dpi. |

## Regions

| Slot | Surface |
| --- | --- |
| `<VanMockup.CurbSide>` | Curb-side (+Z) wrap panel (bare children). |
| `<VanMockup.StreetSide>` | Street-side (−Z) wrap panel. |
| `<VanMockup.Rear>` | Rear doors: the between-the-taillights panel, or the whole barn-door face with `coverage="full"`. |
| `<VanMockup.LicensePlate>` | Front and rear plates. A string renders the built-in plate face; a node renders as-is. |

Every slot takes per-surface overrides — `surfaceBackground`,
`resolution`, `surfaceStyle` — over the mockup-level defaults. The
same slots exist on the bare `<Van>`.

With the default `coverage="panel"` all three wrap panels sit clear of the
wheel arches, lights and cab glass. Rotate the group to pose the van; the
panels stay live at any angle.

## More examples

### Full-coverage wrap with carve-outs

`coverage="full"` stretches the live panels across the entire side elevation
— rockers to roofline, tail to nose — and across the whole barn-door rear
face. A CSS `clip-path` (computed from the same dimension spec the geometry
is built from) carves the body outline, wheel arches, door glass and rear
taillight clusters out of the live panels, and the sides composite per-pixel
against a depth occluder shaped like that clip — so the wheels and glass
show through the carves while the proud hardware (mirrors, door handles,
the sliding-door track, the cab-door shut lines) draws over your livery,
the way remounted hardware sits on real vinyl.

<ObjectDemo demo="van-full" />

```tsx
<VanMockup coverage="full" rotation={[0, -0.42, 0]}>
  <FullLivery />
  <VanMockup.StreetSide><FullLivery /></VanMockup.StreetSide>
  <VanMockup.Rear><RearLivery /></VanMockup.Rear>
</VanMockup>
```
