`<AppleWatch>` and `<AppleWatchMockup>` render an Apple Watch - the Series 11,
the Series 12 or the Ultra 4, chosen with `variant` - wearing a real wristband
on an invisible wrist directly behind the case: the band sliding into the
slots machined in the case edges, exactly how worn-band product photos pose
it.

The Series case is a 46 mm squircle with the finely knurled Digital Crown, the
flush side button in its machined recess, a drilled mic hole and two speaker
slots on the left edge, under an edge-to-edge crystal over the live 208×248
rounded-rect face. The Ultra 4 is the 49 mm titanium case: barrel flanks under
a flat raised lip, tighter corners, a flat crystal over a 211×257 face, a raised
crown guard on the right flank shielding a larger, coarsely lobed crown and the
side button, and on the left the speaker grille, the orange Action button and
the siren port - the button orange whatever the case finish, because on the
hardware it is. Every back carries its real sensor cluster: an optical heart
sensor behind a round crystal ringed by the ECG electrode - sunk flush into a
body-colour plate on the Series, raised on a shallow black ceramic dome on the
Ultra - with the charging-coil ring engraved outside it.

The Series wear the **Solo Loop**: one continuous stretchy band, sized to the
wrist rather than adjusted, so there is no closure, no adjustment holes and no
hardware anywhere on it - it just flares into the lug slots at both ends. The
Ultra wears its **Ocean Band**: a 24 mm rubber strap with a moulded ridge every
6.7 mm and stadium holes in the troughs, closing with a titanium buckle and
loop in the case's finish that wrap round the strap's edges. Neither unfastens
here, which is why this component has no `bandOpen` prop. For a band that lays open, see [Galaxy Watch](/docs/api/galaxy-watch-8).

## Props

Beyond the [shared screen props](/docs/screen-content):

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `variant` | `'series11' \| 'series12' \| 'ultra4'` | `'series11'` | Which Apple Watch to render. The Series 12 is the Series 11's case a millimetre wider, with the generation's finishes; the Ultra 4 is the 49 mm titanium case with the crown guard and the Action button. |
| `color` | `string` | `'#1c1d21'` | Case. Takes a retail colorway id from `APPLE_WATCH_COLORWAYS[variant]` (Series 11 `jetblack`, `spacegray`, `rosegold`, `silver`; Series 12 `darkbronze`, `lightgold`, `black`, `spacegray`, `radiantgold`, `natural`, `pearlwhite`, `nightblue`; Ultra 4 `natural`, `black`) or any CSS color. |
| `bandColor` | `string` | `'#2a2c31'` | Band colorway - the Solo Loop on the Series, the Ocean Band on the Ultra. |
| `resolution` | `number` | per variant | Virtual display width - each model's own point grid (208 on the Series, 211 on the Ultra), so watchOS-sized layouts apply. |

There is no `orientation` prop, and no `bandOpen`.

## The models

### Apple Watch Ultra 4

The 49 mm grade 5 titanium case the Ultra 3 introduced: barrel flanks under a
flat raised lip, tighter corners, a flat sapphire crystal over the 1.98″
422×514 panel (211×257 logical), the crown guard with the orange-ringed,
coarsely lobed crown, the orange Action button beside the ten-hole speaker
grille, the black ceramic sensor dome, and the ridged Ocean Band. Apple's 44 mm width takes in the guard and
crown; the case itself is 41.4 mm across, per Apple's bezel drawing. Retail finishes: `natural`, `black`.
