`<GalaxyWatch>` and `<GalaxyWatchMockup>` render a Samsung Galaxy Watch 8,
44 mm, wearing a real wristband on an invisible wrist directly behind the case -
two domed, tapering straps meeting at a closure on the underside of the wrist,
matching how worn-band product photos pose it.

The case is the aluminium "cushion" squircle carrying a **fully round** 1.47"
display raised on its dial puck, so the metal stays visible around the live
circular 240×240 face. Two raised chamfered keys straddle the mic hole on the
right edge, with twin machined speaker slots on the left. The back carries the
BioActive sensor puck standing proud of the aluminium, an optical heart sensor
behind its round crystal ringed by the ECG electrode.

Its band is the Dynamic-Lug-style two-strap band: nearly case-wide at the
connector, tapering around the wrist to a stainless pin buckle whose tongue
rises through one of the adjustment holes punched clean through the long strap,
with a keeper holding the tail down. The lengths come from the retail fit range
rather than being eyeballed.

## Props

Beyond the [shared screen props](/docs/screen-content):

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `variant` | `'watch8'` | `'watch8'` | Which Galaxy Watch to render. |
| `color` | `string` | `'#1c1d21'` | Aluminium case. Takes a retail colorway id from `GALAXY_WATCH_COLORWAYS` (`graphite`, `silver`) or any CSS color. |
| `bandColor` | `string` | `'#2a2c31'` | Strap colorway (fluoroelastomer). |
| `bandOpen` | `boolean` | `false` | Lay the band out unbuckled and flat instead of wearing it. |
| `resolution` | `number` | `240` | Virtual display width - 240 is the watch's own dp grid, and the display is round, so give your face a `border-radius: 50%` root (or design edge-to-edge and let the crystal mask it). |

There is no `orientation` prop.

## More examples

### Unbuckled, showing the sensor back

A worn band is a closed loop, and the far side of a closed loop sits between
the camera and the watch - so it always hides the case back. `bandOpen` runs
both straps straight out from the case instead, so the hole row, the buckle and
the BioActive sensor back all face the viewer, the way a band is photographed
off-wrist. The mockup backs the camera off to frame the full length.
