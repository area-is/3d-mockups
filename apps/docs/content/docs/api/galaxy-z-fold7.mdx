`<Fold>` renders a Galaxy Z Fold 7; `<FoldMockup>` is the all-in-one version.
One device, two form factors: **open** (default) fills your content onto the
large, nearly square inner display - a faint crease runs down its center - and
**closed** (`open={false}`) folds to the tall cover display with the rear
triple camera on the back.

<ObjectDemo demo="fold-open" checker />

```tsx
<FoldMockup color="#c9ccce" frameColor="#b9bcbe">
  <YourApp /> {/* fills the big inner display */}
</FoldMockup>
```

## Props

Beyond the [shared screen props](/docs/screen-content) and, on the mockup, the
[shared mockup props](/docs/api):

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `open` | `boolean` | `true` | Unfolded tablet (content on the big inner display, with a center crease) versus the folded candy-bar (content on the tall cover display, rear triple camera showing). |
| `openAngle` | `number` | – | Degree of openness (0 = shut, 180 = flat), overriding `open`. Intermediate angles render the real Flex Mode book pose: the panels pivot around the Armor FlexHinge while its rounded spine (with the SAMSUNG engraving) stays tangent to both back shells, wrapping the fold at every angle, and your content bends across the crease on two composited planes. The pose is continuous from nearly shut to nearly flat - only ~0° snaps to the dedicated folded pose, ~177°+ to the flat-open one. |
| `orientation` | `'portrait' \| 'landscape'` | `'portrait'` | Landscape lays the device on its side and swaps the virtual display with upright content. |
| `color` | `string` | `'#3a3d42'` | Back panel. Takes a retail colorway id from `FOLD_COLORWAYS`, which also presets `frameColor`, or any CSS color. |
| `frameColor` | `string` | `'#54585f'` | Metal frame, buttons, hinge, and camera rings. |
| `resolution` | `number` | per state | CSS pixel width of the active display; height follows the panel aspect. Defaults to the inner display when open (820×910) and the cover display when closed (360×835). |

## The two states

The active display is whichever screen is showing, so your content maps onto
the inner display when open and the cover display when closed. Switch with a
single prop:

<ObjectDemo demo="fold-closed" />

```tsx
<FoldMockup open={false} autoRotate float color="#3a3d42" frameColor="#54585f" surfaceBackground="#000">
  <LockScreen /> {/* now on the tall cover screen */}
</FoldMockup>
```

## Any angle in between

`openAngle` poses the book at any degree of openness - the half-open standing
pose with the rounded spine wrapping the fold and your content bending across
the crease:

<ObjectDemo demo="fold-flex" />

```tsx
<FoldMockup openAngle={110} color="blueshadow">
  <YourApp />
</FoldMockup>
```

## Colorways

Try Silver Shadow `#c9ccce` with `frameColor="#b9bcbe"`, Blue Shadow `#4a5568`,
or Jet Black `#26282c` with `frameColor="#3a3d42"`.
