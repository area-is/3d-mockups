title: Milk carton
description: A gable-top beverage carton with every wall and both roof panels printable.
---

`<MilkCarton>` and `<MilkCartonMockup>` render a gable-top beverage carton
(95×241×95 mm, the US half-gallon): poly-coated board walls, the roof folded
up to a ridge, the sealed fin standing on top, and a ribbed screw cap moulded
onto the front roof panel. All four walls are live, and so are both roof panels -
the cap rides *over* the front one, exactly like a real spout rides over the
print. Pass `size` (in millimeters) for a quart, a half-pint or anything
else; `height` is the overall height, roof and fin included.

The ends are not flat triangles, because on a real carton they cannot be: a
side panel carries its full depth up past the eave while the roof narrows
toward the ridge, so the excess board creases down the middle and folds
inward. Each end here is that ear fold - two facets meeting along a crease
that is flush with the wall at the eave and dives in the whole way up, until
the halves close on each other just under the fin and are pinched flat to be
sealed into it.

## Props

Beyond the [shared screen props](/docs/screen-content):

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `size` | `{ width, height, depth }` | `95×241×95` | Real size in **millimeters**, `height` measured over the roof and fin. The longest edge normalizes to the stage, so any size fills the camera at true proportions. |
| `color` | `string` | `'#f4f3ef'` | Board color. Poly-coated white by default; try a brand dip or kraft. |
| `capColor` | `string` | `'#d7dbdf'` | Screw cap color. |
| `cap` | `boolean` | `true` | Render the screw cap. Off gives the older pull-open spout carton. |
| `surfaceBackground` | `string` | `'#ffffff'` | CSS background behind each printed panel. |
| `resolution` | `number` | `420` | Virtual front panel width in CSS px; other panels share its dpi. |

## Regions

| Slot | Panel |
| --- | --- |
| `<MilkCartonMockup.Front>` | Front wall - the hero face (bare children). |
| `<MilkCartonMockup.Back>` | Back wall. |
| `<MilkCartonMockup.Right>` | Right wall. |
| `<MilkCartonMockup.Left>` | Left wall. |
| `<MilkCartonMockup.GableFront>` | Front roof panel, with the screw cap riding over it. |
| `<MilkCartonMockup.GableBack>` | Back roof panel. |

Every slot takes per-surface overrides - `surfaceBackground`,
`resolution`, `surfaceStyle` - over the mockup-level defaults. The
same slots exist on the bare `<MilkCarton>`.
