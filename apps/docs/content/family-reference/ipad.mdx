`<IPad>` and `<IPadMockup>` cover the current Apple iPad lineup at true
relative sizes - iPad Pro (M5), iPad Air (M4) and the standard iPad (A16).
Each variant gets its real hardware: the Pro's camera pod with LiDAR and
flash, the Air's and iPad's bare single lens with the Touch ID top button, the
Apple Pencil charging strip on the long edge, the Smart Connector dots (on the
back, or sunk into the rail on the A16), the Apple glyph as real vector
geometry centered on the back, the model wordmark below it, drilled speaker
clusters in both short edges, and the front camera in the landscape-top bezel.

The virtual display swaps to 1376×1032 in landscape - real desktop-class
layouts apply.

## Props

Beyond the [shared screen props](/docs/screen-content):

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `variant` | `'ipadpro13' \| 'ipadpro11' \| 'ipadair13' \| 'ipadair11' \| 'ipad11'` | `'ipadpro13'` | Which iPad: Pro 13"/11" (camera pod, Pencil strip, Face ID), Air 13"/11" (single lens, Touch ID top button), or the A16 iPad (single lens, edge Smart Connector). |
| `orientation` | `'portrait' \| 'landscape'` | `'portrait'` | Landscape lays the tablet on its side and swaps the virtual display (for example 1376×1032 on the 13" Pro) with upright content. |
| `resolution` | `number` | per variant | Defaults to the variant's logical grid: 1032×1376 (ipadpro13), 834×1210 (ipadpro11), 1024×1366 (ipadair13), 820×1180 (ipadair11, ipad11). |
| `color` | `string` | per variant | Body color. Takes a retail colorway id from `IPAD_COLORWAYS` - Pro `spaceblack`/`silver`, Air `spacegray`/`starlight`/`purple`/`blue`, iPad `silver`/`blue`/`pink`/`yellow` - or any CSS color. |
