`<GalaxyWatch>` and `<GalaxyWatchMockup>` render a Samsung Galaxy Watch - the
Watch 8, the Watch 9 or the Watch Ultra 2, chosen with `variant` - wearing a
real wristband on an invisible wrist directly behind the case: two domed
straps leaving the lugs halfway up the case and meeting at a closure on the
underside of the wrist, matching how worn-band product photos pose it.

Every model is the "cushion" squircle carrying a **fully round** display
raised on its dial puck, so the metal stays visible around the live circular
face. The 44 mm Watch 8 and Watch 9 are aluminium with two raised chamfered
keys straddling the mic hole; the 47 mm Watch Ultra 2 is titanium, deeper,
with a three-key run ending in its orange Quick Button - orange whatever the
case finish, because on the hardware it is. The back carries the BioActive
sensor: a polished metal puck standing proud of the case - the ECG electrode,
split across the middle - round a small dark window with the LEDs and diamond
photodiodes, the model line engraved round it, a vent, four tri-wing screws
and a band release by each lug. On the Ultra 2 the puck sits on a darker
round plate, and the releases are copper.

The band is the Sport Band, measured off Samsung's laid-flat render: a 21.8 mm
strap of constant width flaring only a little into the Dynamic Lug, closing
with a buckle in the case's metal - a frame wider than the strap, wrapped
round its edges, with its tongue in one of the ten holes punched clean through
the long strap - and a keeper moulded into the strap behind it holding the
tail down. The lengths come from the retail fit range rather than being
eyeballed; the Ultra 2 wears it at its wider strap width, with a titanium
keeper.

## Props

Beyond the [shared screen props](/docs/screen-content) and, on the mockup, the
[shared mockup props](/docs/api):

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `variant` | `'watch8' \| 'watch9' \| 'watchultra2'` | `'watch8'` | Which Galaxy Watch to render. The Watch 9 is the Watch 8's case with the generation's internals; the Watch Ultra 2 is the 47 mm titanium squircle. |
| `color` | `string` | `'#1c1d21'` | Case. Takes a colorway id from `GALAXY_WATCH_COLORWAYS[variant]` (see [Colorways](#colorways)) or any CSS color. |
| `bandColor` | `string` | `'#2a2c31'` | Strap colorway (fluoroelastomer). |
| `bandOpen` | `boolean` | `false` | Lay the band out unbuckled and flat instead of wearing it. |
| `resolution` | `number` | per variant | Virtual display width - each model's own dp grid (240 on the 44 mm models, 249 on the Ultra 2), and the display is round, so give your face a `border-radius: 50%` root (or design edge-to-edge and let the crystal mask it). |

There is no `orientation` prop: a watch has one pose.

## Regions

One region: the round screen (bare children). Use the explicit
`<GalaxyWatchMockup.Screen>` slot when you want per-surface overrides -
`surfaceBackground`, `resolution`, `surfaceStyle`. The same slot exists on
the bare `<GalaxyWatch>`.

## Colorways

Pass a colorway id from `GALAXY_WATCH_COLORWAYS[variant]` for that model's
retail case finish, or any CSS color for a custom one. The catalog covers the
case; `bandColor` takes any CSS color. An id wins over a CSS color of the same
name - pass hex if you mean the CSS one.

| Model | Colorway ids (body color) |
| --- | --- |
| Galaxy Watch 8, Galaxy Watch 9 (`watch8`, `watch9`) | `graphite` (`#33363c`), `silver` (`#d9dbde`) |
| Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 (`watchultra2`) | `titaniumsilver` (`#ccced3`), `titaniumgray` (`#8b8e94`) |

### Unbuckled, showing the sensor back

A worn band is a closed loop, and the far side of a closed loop sits between
the camera and the watch - so it always hides the case back. `bandOpen` runs
both straps straight out from the case instead, so the hole row, the buckle and
the BioActive sensor back all face the viewer, the way a band is photographed
off-wrist. The mockup backs the camera off to frame the full length.
