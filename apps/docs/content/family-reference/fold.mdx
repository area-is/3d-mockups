`<Fold>` renders a Galaxy Z Fold - the Z Fold 7, the wide Z Fold 8 or the
Z Fold 8 Ultra, chosen with `variant`; `<FoldMockup>` is the all-in-one
version. One device, two form factors: **open** (default) fills your content
onto the large inner display - a faint crease runs down its center - and
**closed** (`openAngle={false}`) folds to the cover display with the rear
camera pill on the back.

// The same model with no stage of its own. Inside a canvas you arrange

## Props

Beyond the [shared screen props](/docs/screen-content) and, on the mockup, the
[shared mockup props](/docs/api):

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `variant` | `'fold7' \| 'fold8' \| 'fold8ultra'` | `'fold7'` | Which Galaxy Z Fold to render. `fold7` and `fold8ultra` share the tall book chassis; `fold8` is the generation's wide form factor, opening into a landscape 4:3 tablet. |
| `openAngle` | `boolean \| number` | `true` | How far the book is open. `true` is the unfolded tablet (content on the big inner display, with a center crease), `false` the folded candy-bar (content on the cover display, rear camera pill showing). A number is degrees (0 = shut, 180 = flat) and renders the real Flex Mode book pose: the camera half holds still and the cover half swings open about the crease, the Armor FlexHinge, whose rounded spine (with the SAMSUNG engraving) stays tangent to both back shells, wrapping the fold at every angle, and your content bends across the crease on two composited planes. The pose is continuous: every angle renders its own pose, and only the extremes hand over to the dedicated folded (~0°) and flat (180°) poses, which draw the crease as one seamless screen. The crease is the one line that never moves, so the open device is centred on it and the folded one lies on the camera half, half its own width to the right. |
| `orientation` | `'portrait' \| 'landscape'` | `'portrait'` | Rotates the device a quarter turn and swaps the virtual display with upright content. On the fold7 and fold8ultra that lays the tablet on its side; on the natively landscape fold8 it stands it upright. |
| `statusBar` | `boolean \| StatusBarOption` | `false` | Draw the system status bar across the top of the screen. `true` gives the platform's defaults - One UI's, on the cover screen and on the inner display alike, clearing whichever punch hole is showing - including the inner display's off-centre one; an object sets the clock, the meters and the ink (`{{ time: '14:05', battery: 0.42, batteryPercent: true, color: '#1c1c1e' }}`). It is placed from this device's own camera cutout and logical grid, so it lines up with the hardware on every variant. See [Status bar](/docs/screen-content#status-bar). |
| `color` | `string` | `'#3a3d42'` | Back panel. Takes a colorway id from `FOLD_COLORWAYS[variant]` (see [Colorways](#colorways)), which brings that model's frame and hinge with it, or any CSS color - the metal is then derived from it. |
| `resolution` | `number` | per state | CSS pixel width of the active display; height follows the panel aspect. Defaults to the device's own logical grid for whichever screen is showing - see the [device table](/docs/devices). |
| `coverScreenUntil` | `number` | `30` | The hinge angle, in degrees, up to which the cover display stays lit as the device opens; above it your content moves to the inner display, so one of the two is lit at every angle, as on the hardware. `30` is where Android's reference foldable swaps; `90` keeps the cover on through the half-open tent pose, `0` swaps the moment the hinge moves. |

## The two states

The active display is whichever screen is lit, so your content maps onto the
cover display from shut until the hinge passes `coverScreenUntil` (30° by
default) and the inner display after it. Switch with a single prop:

<MockupExplorer
  component="FoldMockup"
  props={{ openAngle: false, autoRotate: true, float: true, color: '#3a3d42', surfaceBackground: '#000' }}
/>

## Any angle in between

`openAngle` takes a number of degrees to pose the book at any openness - the half-open standing
pose with the rounded spine wrapping the fold and your content bending across
the crease:

<MockupExplorer
  component="FoldMockup"
  props={{ openAngle: 110, color: '#47536b', rotation: [0, -0.35, 0] }}
/>

Between open and closed, once the inner display is lit (past
`coverScreenUntil`), each half of it is its own screen, so your screen content
is mounted once per half: effects, state and video run twice. Fully open,
shut, or while the cover display is lit, it is mounted once. Each half lays
the content out at the whole display's size and shows its own part of it, so
`useSurface()` reports the whole display in both halves - the same numbers as
open flat.

## Regions

One region: the screen (bare children). `Screen` follows whichever display is
lit - the cover display from shut until the hinge passes `coverScreenUntil`,
the inner display after it. Use the explicit `<FoldMockup.Screen>` slot when
you want per-surface overrides - `surfaceBackground`, `resolution`,
`surfaceStyle`. The same slot exists on the bare `<Fold>`.

## Colorways

Pass a colorway id from `FOLD_COLORWAYS[variant]` for that model's retail
finish; it brings the matching frame and hinge with it. Any CSS color works for
a custom finish, and the metal is then derived from it, so `color="#4a5568"`
needs nothing else. An id wins over a CSS color of the same name - pass hex if
you mean the CSS one.

| Model | Colorway ids (body color) |
| --- | --- |
| Galaxy Z Fold 7 (`fold7`) | `blueshadow` (`#47536b`), `jetblack` (`#17181c`), `silvershadow` (`#c9ccce`), `mint` (`#cfe0d2`) |
| Galaxy Z Fold 8 (`fold8`) | `lavender` (`#c9c2d9`), `cream` (`#ece5d8`), `graphite` (`#45474c`), `pistachio` (`#d4dfc0`) |
| Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra (`fold8ultra`) | `violetshadow` (`#5c5670`), `cream` (`#ece5d8`), `graphite` (`#45474c`), `greenshadow` (`#4a5a50`) |

## This model

### Galaxy Z Fold 7

The tall book: a 6.5″ 21:9 cover display folding open into the nearly square
8.0″ inner panel (820×910), with the vertical triple-camera pill top-right on
the back. Retail finishes: `blueshadow`, `jetblack`, `silvershadow`, `mint`.

### Galaxy Z Fold 8

The wide one. Folded it is a short, broad bar with a 5.5″ cover display
(480×758); unfolded it is **wider than it is tall** - a landscape 4:3 tablet
whose 7.6″ inner panel renders 1020×770 in the default pose, so design for a
landscape viewport when it is open. `orientation="landscape"` stands it
upright into 770×1020 instead. Retail finishes: `lavender`, `cream`,
`graphite`, `pistachio`.

### Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra

The Fold 7's chassis with the generation's internals: the same footprints
folded and open (a tenth of a millimetre thinner unfolded), the same 8.0″
inner diagonal on a sharper 2504×2256 panel that keeps the 820×910 logical
grid, and the 200 MP triple camera. Retail finishes: `violetshadow`, `cream`,
`graphite`, `greenshadow`.
