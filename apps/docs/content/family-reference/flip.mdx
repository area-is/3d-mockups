`<Flip>` renders a Galaxy Z Flip 7; `<FlipMockup>` is the all-in-one version.
One device, two form factors: **open** (default) fills your content onto the
tall 6.85″ main display, and **closed** (`open={false}`) folds to the compact
square whose front is nearly all cover screen - with the two separately
protruding lens rings and flash sitting on the glass (no plate joins them,
matching the retail hardware), and the hinge band (complete with its
engraved wordmark) capping the bottom. Detail geometry (button pills, lens
rings, ports, seams) follows a reference scan of the retail device checked
against the official product photography.

<ObjectDemo demo="flip-open" checker />

```tsx
<FlipMockup color="coralred">
  <YourApp /> {/* fills the tall main display */}
</FlipMockup>
```

## Props

Beyond the [shared screen props](/docs/screen-content) and, on the mockup, the
[shared mockup props](/docs/api):

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `open` | `boolean` | `true` | Unfolded tall phone (content on the 360×837 main display) versus the folded compact (content on the nearly square 316×353 cover display, lens rings overlaid like cutouts). |
| `openAngle` | `number` | – | Degree of openness (0 = shut, 180 = flat), overriding `open`. Intermediate angles render the real Flex Mode pose: the halves pivot around the Armor FlexHinge while its glossy spine band (with the SAMSUNG engraving) rolls into the gap, and your content bends across the fold on two composited planes. The pose is continuous from nearly shut to nearly flat - only ~0° snaps to the dedicated folded pose, ~177°+ to the flat-open one. |
| `orientation` | `'portrait' \| 'landscape'` | `'portrait'` | Landscape lays the device on its side and swaps the virtual display with upright content. |
| `color` | `string` | `'#22252b'` | Back glass / cover. Takes a retail colorway id from `FLIP_COLORWAYS`, which brings that model's measured frame and hinge band with it, or any CSS color - the metal is then derived from it. |
| `resolution` | `number` | per state | CSS pixel width of the active display; height follows the panel aspect. |

## The two states

Your content maps onto whichever screen is showing. Folded, the cover screen
wraps around the cameras exactly like the real FlexWindow:

<ObjectDemo demo="flip-closed" />

```tsx
<FlipMockup open={false} autoRotate float color="coralred" surfaceBackground="#000">
  <LockScreen /> {/* now on the square cover screen */}
</FlipMockup>
```

## Any angle in between

`openAngle` poses the phone at any degree of openness - the classic half-open
Flex Mode stance, with the hinge spine rolling into the fold and your content
bending across it:

<ObjectDemo demo="flip-flex" />

```tsx
<FlipMockup openAngle={100} color="blueshadow">
  <YourApp />
</FlipMockup>
```

## Colorways

Pass an id for the retail finish - `color="coralred"`, `color="blueshadow"`,
`color="jetblack"`, `color="mint"` - and the frame and hinge band come with it.
A CSS color gets metal derived from it instead, so `color="#bfe3d0"` needs
nothing else.
